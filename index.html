Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of structuring your content and online presence so that AI-powered platforms — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's AI Overviews, and similar — cite, recommend, and surface your brand in their generated answers. As a growing share of search journeys now begin with or pass through these tools, being absent from AI-generated answers is increasingly costly for B2B pipeline generation. GEO doesn't replace SEO — it extends it into the new layer of discovery.
